Community UX testing · 2019–2020

Testla:
speed-dating for user testing.

Testla was a speed-dating style UX testing community in Hong Kong, created to help startups quickly collect feedback from real users.

TESTLA

Testla overview

What if startups could help each other test their products?

Many startups didn’t fully understand the value of user research, or didn’t have the budget for formal user testing.

I teamed up with a friend and launched Testla in early 2020 to experiment with this idea.

The result

In just over a year, 200+ people tested together.

It was especially rewarding to see participants return as testers for other products after having their own tested.

01

Background

Recruiting testers was the hard part.

As a UX designer in startups, I always wanted user research in my workflow. At one point I tried recruiting participants on the street or in subway stations. On a lucky day I found one or two people, but it was inefficient and hard to find the right participants.

02

How it works

Rotating stations, 15 minutes each.

Each event brought together product owners and testers.

01

Set up

Product owners set up a testing station for their product.

02

Rotate

Testers rotate between stations to try different products.

03

Learn

Each 15-minute session lets owners test with 5 users, and testers try 5 products.

The format let startups gather quick feedback while creating a collaborative learning environment.

03

Reflection

What I enjoyed.

  • Solving a real problem I faced in my day-to-day work
  • Meeting UX designers and founders outside my usual network
  • Supporting and contributing to the local UX community

What I hope to do next

  • Bring back in-person testing events when possible
  • Keep creating opportunities for designers and startups to collaborate
Impact

A community built around testing.

Through these sessions we met many talented UX designers, product managers and startup founders.
