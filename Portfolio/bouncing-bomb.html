Bouncing Bomb:
AI-informed, human-validated.

As a board game enthusiast, I explored how far AI tools like ChatGPT, Lovable and Replit could take a simple card game idea, from scratch to a playable prototype.

BOMB

Bouncing Bomb overview

A hybrid testing workflow that combines AI iteration with real playtesting.

With AI-generated rules and card lists as a foundation, I ran human playtesting sessions with my board game group.

Feedback was immediate and emotional: players flagged pacing issues, confusion with reverse cards, and unclear win conditions, none of which AI alone had caught.

Workflow

From sketch to playtest.

I mapped a high-level workflow to track how ideas moved from sketch → AI generation → digital prototype → physical cards → playtesting.

02

Sketch

Starting with the core mechanics.

I sketched the core mechanics of how the game might play. These rough ideas became the starting point for AI-powered iteration.

03

Game rules

Iterating the rules with ChatGPT.

Using ChatGPT, I ran multiple iterations to refine the game loop, the number of cards for 3–8 players, and the balance of the mechanics. This became Version 1 of the rules.

04

AI prototype

Playtesting online.

I translated the rules into digital prototypes with Replit and Lovable. Replit came closest to what I envisioned, letting me playtest online.

05

Physical cards

A deck for the table.

I designed simple card layouts in Figma, printed them, and made a physical deck for in-person playtesting.

06

User feedback

What players noticed that AI didn’t.

01

Rounds dragged

Too many “fluffy bomb” cards slowed the game down.

02

Reverse cards confused

New players found the reverse cards hard to follow.

03

Strong potential

The game felt promising, but needed tweaks before the next round.

Next, I’ll create Version 2 to reduce confusion and streamline gameplay, then run more playtests.

What I learned

AI and humans in one feedback loop.

Instead of treating AI and human testing separately, I combined them: player insights informed new AI prompts, which refined rules, rebalanced probabilities and tweaked mechanics more efficiently.
